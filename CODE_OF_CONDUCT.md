# Code of Conduct

We want every Aback project to be a welcoming, respectful place to contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hi@aback.group**. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

This code applies in all project spaces, and in public spaces when someone is representing an Aback project.
