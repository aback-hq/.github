# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Aback Group's products and users safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **hi@aback.group** with the subject line `Security report`. Please include:

- the product and URL (or repository) affected
- what the issue is and what an attacker could do with it
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- your name or handle, if you would like to be credited

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem, and do not test against other people's accounts or data.

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report within 3 working days.
- We will keep you updated while we investigate and fix it.
- Once it is fixed, we will credit you if you would like us to.

## Scope

This policy covers the products and websites listed on [aback.group](https://aback.group) and the repositories in this organization. Individual products may publish their own policy; when they do, that policy takes precedence.
